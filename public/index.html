Food Safety & Partner Trust

Slide 06: Food safety comes first. The platform supports coordination; it does not replace food-safety checks or local regulations.

🍲

Safe Food Only

Restaurants should list only food that has been handled and stored safely and is suitable for donation. Food must be unconsumed, clean, and stored in hygienic containers.

  • Cooked meals kept either hot (>65°C) or chilled (<4°C).
  • No food that has been served on customer tables or buffet customer plates.
  • Packaged goods within safe shelf life with intact tamper seals.
⏱️

Clear Time Limits

Every listing includes preparation details and a collection deadline. Expired listings are automatically retired to prevent stale pickups.

  • Max 2–4 hours pickup window for hot cooked food.
  • Best-before deadline mandatory for every single listing.
  • Auto-flagging when pickup deadline has elapsed.
🛡️

Verified Partners

Restaurant and NGO profiles and contact details are vetted before they can publish or claim surplus food.

  • FSSAI / Food Authority registration checks for restaurants.
  • Registered NGO Trust deeds / 12A / 80G validation.
  • Dedicated coordinator phone numbers verified on dispatch.
⚠️

Important Regulatory & Compliance Notice (Slide 06)

Always follow applicable food-safety laws, municipal health guidelines, and local regulations. Never distribute food that looks, smells, or is suspected of being unsafe. Both restaurants and NGOs maintain responsibility for physical inspection at the point of handoff.
